TERRATILLS
USER DASHBOARD
Pages, Functional Details, Navigation and Implementation Prompt
Functional specification


1. Product-wide dashboard rules
* One account and one unified dashboard for every user. Do not create mutually exclusive account types such as Farmer, Labourer, Landowner, Machinery Owner or Customer.
* A user can offer several services and request services from others using the same account. Listings are user-owned records; one user may own many listings across categories.
* Customer and provider are contextual roles for an individual request or booking, not permanent account types. The same user may be a customer in one booking and a provider in another.
* Initial categories: Farmland Leasing, Machinery Rental, Farm Labour, and Agricultural Training/Tutorials. The system must allow additional categories.
* Administrative/staff permissions are separate and are not part of the ordinary user dashboard.
* Backend authorisation is mandatory for every private record and action. Hiding a frontend control is not sufficient.
* Booking status, payment status and settlement status are separate state machines.
* A booking request is not a paid or confirmed booking. Provider acceptance and agreement on scope, date, price and terms precede payment.
* Payment success must be verified server-side through the payment provider's supported mechanisms. A browser redirect is not proof of payment.
* Do not call the payment arrangement escrow unless the provider and legal structure explicitly support that term.
* AI may assist discovery, extraction, estimates and guidance. It must not approve identity/ownership, certify machinery, approve providers, initiate payments, confirm completion, change authoritative booking status or release funds.
* Bach is the selected payment provider. Confirm its actual API, payment, webhook, settlement, transfer, refund and payout capabilities before implementing dependent financial features.

Detailed page specifications
3.1 Dashboard Overview
ROUTE/dashboardPRESENTATIONFull pagePurpose: Provide a consolidated view of the user's activity and next actions.
* Welcome section: time-appropriate greeting, display name, profile image and profile completion.
* Verification status and link to Verification Centre when action is needed.
* Summary metrics: total bookings, active bookings, services listed, incoming requests, provider earnings and pending payouts. Customer spending must not be counted as earnings.
* Quick actions: Explore Services, Add a Service, My Bookings, Incoming Requests, Messages, Earnings and Support.
* Recent bookings: reference, service, other participant, booking date, scheduled date, amount, booking status and payment status.
* My service listings: image, title, category, location, price, status, availability and management actions.
* Incoming requests: customer, listing, requested date, location, proposed amount, status and response action.
* Recent transactions and notifications with references, dates, amounts/statuses and links to records.
* AI assistant shortcut with suggested agricultural/marketplace questions.
Links and behaviour: Explore opens Explore Services; Add a Service opens its form; bookings, requests, listings, transactions and notifications open their exact records; finance summaries open Earnings/Payouts; assistant opens AI Agricultural Assistant.
3.2 Explore Services
ROUTE/dashboard/explorePRESENTATIONFull page; filters may use a drawerPurpose: Allow users to discover services available on TerraTills.
* Search by service name, keyword, category, machinery type, labour type, training subject or location.
* Categories: Farmland Leasing, Machinery Rental, Farm Labour, Agricultural Training/Tutorials and later enabled categories.
* General filters: state, LGA, town/community, supported distance radius, price, availability, rating, verified provider and date listed.
* Featured/recommended listing summaries: image, title, category, provider, verification, location, price basis, rating/review count, availability and save action.
* Recently viewed listings where view history is supported; AI-assisted search entry point grounded in real listings.
* Loading, empty, no-results, network-error and clear-filter states.
Links and behaviour: Category opens Service Category; search opens Search Results; listing opens Listing Details; save updates Saved Items; provider opens User Profile; AI search opens Assistant.
3.3 Search Results
ROUTE/dashboard/explore/searchPRESENTATIONFull page; filters may use a drawerPurpose: Display results matching the search query.
* Show editable query, result count, active filter chips, sorting and listing results.
* Sort by relevance, newest, lowest/highest price and highest rated where rating data is sufficient.
* Filters include category, location, price, availability, provider verification and category-specific attributes.
* Support pagination/load-more, clear filters, no-result suggestions and error/retry states.
* Preserve query, filters, sort order and page/scroll context when opening a listing and returning.
Links and behaviour: A result opens Listing Details; category filters open Service Category; save updates Saved Items.
3.4 Service Category
ROUTE/dashboard/explore/category/:slugPRESENTATIONFull page; filter controls may use a drawerPurpose: Present listings and relevant filters for one service category.
* Common contents: category title/description, search within category, location, price, availability, verification and rating filters, sort, listing results and related categories.
* Farmland attributes: size/unit, location, known soil details, land-use suitability, water/irrigation, access road, lease duration, price, availability and ownership/authority verification state.
* Machinery attributes: type, make/model, condition, capacity/specifications, rental basis, operator availability, delivery, fuel/operating terms, maintenance and available dates.
* Labour attributes: work type, skills, experience, worker count, location, availability, duration, pricing basis, team/individual availability and relevant voluntarily supplied language preferences.
* Training attributes: course title, subject, instructor, format, duration, skill level, price, schedule/self-paced status, materials and certificate availability if offered.
Links and behaviour: Listing opens Listing Details; Add a Service opens the creation form with category preselected; related category opens another category page.
3.5 Listing Details
ROUTE/dashboard/listings/:listingIdPRESENTATIONFull page; image viewer and report flow may be modalPurpose: Provide the information needed to assess a service before booking.
* Gallery: cover image, additional images, captions, image count, full-screen viewer and video support if enabled.
* Listing details: title, category, description, location, price/pricing unit, availability, status, publication date and last update.
* Provider details: display name, profile image, verification, rating/review count, completed bookings if displayed, member-since date and measured response indicators.
* Category-specific information for farmland, machinery, labour or training.
* Price breakdown: base price, unit, quantity/duration assumptions, extra charges, delivery/logistics cost, applicable customer charges and estimated total. Clearly identify estimates and unconfirmed amounts.
* Availability calendar/date selector, unavailable dates and relevant duration/quantity requirements.
* Reviews: rating, count, distribution, comments, dates and provider responses if present.
* Location: state, LGA, town/community, appropriate map location if supplied and coverage/delivery conditions.
* Trust information: provider/listing verification state, applicable checks, customer checklist and report action.
* Related listings and other services by the same provider.
Links and behaviour: Book Now opens Booking Request; Enquiry opens/creates Conversation; save updates Saved Items; provider opens User Profile; report opens report/support flow; related listings open their details.
3.6 Booking Request
ROUTE/dashboard/bookings/new/:listingIdPRESENTATIONFull page multi-step form; short confirmations may be modalPurpose: Collect the information required to request a service.
* Show listing image/title, provider, location, price and pricing unit.
* Collect customer name, phone, email, service location and alternative contact if supported.
* Farmland: intended use, required size, lease start, duration, questions and conditions.
* Machinery: machinery required, work location, date, duration, area/quantity and operator requirement.
* Labour: work type, number of workers, location, start date, duration and expected duties.
* Training: course, participant count, preferred schedule and learning format.
* Collect preferred dates, duration/time, additional work description, special instructions, relevant attachments and delivery/transport requirements.
* Show estimate with price, quantity/duration, extra charges, delivery cost and estimated total; label unconfirmed amounts.
* Show booking terms, cancellation/refund conditions, completion process and required consent.
* Allow review, edit and submit.
Links and behaviour: Submission creates a pending request and opens Booking Confirmation. It appears in My Bookings. User may message provider or return to listing. Submission does not charge the customer.
3.7 Booking Confirmation
ROUTE/dashboard/bookings/:bookingId/confirmationPRESENTATIONFull page or confirmation statePurpose: Confirm successful recording of a booking request.
* Show confirmation, booking reference, service, provider, requested date, estimate/amount, request status and submitted details.
* Explain that the provider must respond and confirm terms before payment.
* Provide Booking Details, provider conversation and Explore Services links.
3.8 My Bookings
ROUTE/dashboard/bookingsPRESENTATIONFull page; status groups are tabs; filters may use drawerPurpose: Manage services requested from other users.
* Summary counts: total, pending, accepted/awaiting payment, paid/active, completed, cancelled and disputed.
* Tabs: All, Pending, Awaiting Payment, Active, Completed, Cancelled and Disputed.
* Each record: image, reference, service/category, provider, booking/scheduled dates, amount, payment status, booking status and last update.
* Filters: status, category, date range, provider, location and payment status.
* State-dependent actions: view, pay where due, message, cancel where permitted, request clarification, confirm completion, dispute where permitted and review after completion.
* Provide empty, loading, error and no-results states.
Links and behaviour: Record opens Booking Details; payment opens payment flow; message opens Conversation; completion opens Booking Completion; eligible completed booking opens Write Review.
3.9 Booking Details
ROUTE/dashboard/bookings/:bookingIdPRESENTATIONFull page; history/summary sections may be collapsiblePurpose: Show complete booking record, agreed terms and current state.
* Header: booking reference, service/category, created date, booking status and separate payment status.
* Timeline: request submitted, provider review, accepted, awaiting payment, payment confirmed, in progress, completion confirmation, settlement and completed. Provide alternate timelines for declined/cancelled/expired/disputed.
* Customer/provider identities and links; service details, location, agreed quantity/duration/dates and instructions.
* Financial details: agreed base amount, extra fees, logistics, total, payment reference/status and refund information.
* Status history with timestamp, actor and explanation; conversation preview and link.
* State-dependent actions: pay, cancel, request modification, contact participant, confirm completion, report issue, dispute and review.
Links and behaviour: Only authorised participants may access the booking. Backend state and permissions determine available actions.
3.10 Booking Messages
ROUTE/dashboard/bookings/:bookingId/messagesPRESENTATIONConversation page; reuse Conversation implementationPurpose: Provide communication linked to one booking.
* Show booking reference, service, participants and message history.
* Support text, timestamps, read indicators, validated attachments and message composer.
* Provide booking summary, Booking Details link and report conversation action.
* Restrict access to booking participants and authorised staff.
3.11 Booking Completion
ROUTE/dashboard/bookings/:bookingId/completionPRESENTATIONFull page or contextual panel/modalPurpose: Capture independent service completion confirmations.
* Customer view: booking summary, provider completion statement/evidence if supplied, Confirm Completion and Report Issue/Dispute actions.
* Provider view: booking summary, Mark Delivered, completion notes/evidence upload and customer confirmation state.
* Record each participant's own confirmation, identity and timestamp.
* States: awaiting provider, awaiting customer, both confirmed, dispute opened, settlement eligible, settlement processing and completed.
* Never allow one participant to confirm on behalf of the other.
3.12 My Services
ROUTE/dashboard/servicesPRESENTATIONFull page; listing statuses are tabsPurpose: Manage every service listing owned by the user.
* Summary: total, active, draft, pending review, paused, rejected and archived.
* Tabs: All, Active, Draft, Pending Review, Paused, Rejected and Archived.
* Listing record: image, title, category, location, price, status, created/updated dates, views/enquiries/requests if tracked and availability.
* Search by title; filter by category, status, date and location.
* Actions: view, edit, pause, activate, delete draft, archive, availability, requests, performance and duplicate if supported.
* Support multiple listings across categories under one account.
Links and behaviour: Add a Service opens creation; listing opens Service Details/Management; edit, availability, performance and requests open corresponding destinations.
3.13 Add Service
ROUTE/dashboard/services/newPRESENTATIONFull page multi-step form; discard confirmation may be modalPurpose: Create and submit a service listing.
* Step 1: select farmland leasing, machinery rental, farm labour, training or another enabled category.
* Step 2: title, description, category/subcategory and service location.
* Step 3: category fields. Farmland: size/unit, location, land use, soil/water/access details, lease terms and relevant evidence. Machinery: type, make/model, condition, specifications, rental basis, operator, delivery and maintenance. Labour: work type, skills, experience, team size, coverage, pricing and availability. Training: course, subject, outcomes, instructor, format, duration, schedule, price, materials and certificate details.
* Step 4: price, currency, unit, minimum quantity/duration, extra charges, delivery fee, fixed/negotiable and quotation requirement.
* Step 5: available/blocked dates, recurring days, lead time and concurrent booking limits.
* Step 6: cover/additional images, optional video, captions, validation and upload progress.
* Step 7: category-relevant verification documents/declarations. Uploading evidence must not mean verification is approved.
* Step 8: preview and edit sections.
* Step 9: save draft, submit for review or publish when permitted.
Links and behaviour: Category determines fields; verification links to Verification Centre; draft returns to My Services; submission opens Service Details/Management with current status.
3.14 Edit Service
ROUTE/dashboard/services/:listingId/editPRESENTATIONFull page multi-step form; destructive confirmations may be modalPurpose: Edit an existing listing owned by the current user.
* Load existing values and category-specific fields.
* Edit description, attributes, price, availability, media and relevant verification details.
* Add/remove images and preview changes; save or cancel.
* Require review for changes where policy requires it.
* Do not rewrite terms on already accepted bookings; preserve booking snapshots.
* Explain why paused/rejected listings cannot be activated until required changes are completed.
3.15 Service Details/Management
ROUTE/dashboard/services/:listingIdPRESENTATIONFull page; management sections may be tabsPurpose: Consolidate management of one listing.
* Show title, category, image, description, location, price, availability, listing status and verification state.
* Show performance summary, incoming requests, recent bookings and reviews where available.
* Actions: edit, pause/activate, view public listing, availability, requests, performance and archive.
* Restrict access to owner and authorised staff.
3.16 Service Availability
ROUTE/dashboard/services/:listingId/availabilityPRESENTATIONFull page or Availability tab; date editor may be modalPurpose: Manage when a service can be booked.
* Calendar of available, blocked, booked and pending-reservation dates where relevant.
* Weekly/monthly views and time slots for time-based services.
* Add available dates, block dates, change times, recurring availability and remove availability.
* Set minimum notice, maximum duration and concurrent booking limits where supported.
* Link dates to bookings and prevent conflicts with server-side availability checks.
3.17 Service Performance
ROUTE/dashboard/services/:listingId/performancePRESENTATIONFull page or Performance tabPurpose: Show measurable listing activity.
* Views, search appearances, enquiries, booking requests, accepted/completed bookings, revenue and reviews only where tracked.
* Date filters, trends and clear metric definitions.
* Show conversion rates only when sufficient underlying data exists.
* Offer evidence-based improvement suggestions without fabricated metrics or promised outcomes.
3.18 Service Requests
ROUTE/dashboard/service-requestsPRESENTATIONFull page; statuses are tabs; filters may use drawerPurpose: Show requests received for the user's listings.
* Summary: new, awaiting response, accepted, declined, expired and converted to bookings.
* Request rows/cards: reference, customer, listing/category, requested date, location, quantity/duration, description, proposed amount, received date, deadline if configured and status.
* Filters by listing, category, status, date and customer.
* Actions: view, accept, decline, ask a question, propose date/price and request more information.
Links and behaviour: Request opens Incoming Request Details; customer opens profile; message opens Conversation; accepted request creates/updates Booking Details.
3.19 Incoming Request Details
ROUTE/dashboard/service-requests/:requestIdPRESENTATIONFull page or detail drawer from request listPurpose: Give the provider information needed to decide on a request.
* Show request reference, customer, listing, category, location, date/duration, quantity, requirements, attachments, price proposal, availability and previous communication.
* Actions: accept, decline, request clarification, message customer, propose alternative date or revised price.
* Acceptance must confirm scope, date, availability, price, additional charges and terms; update/create booking and notify customer.
* Customer must see and agree to final terms before payment.
3.20 Messages
ROUTE/dashboard/messagesPRESENTATIONFull page; split-pane list/detail on wide screensPurpose: Provide a central communication inbox.
* Conversation search/list with participant, avatar, associated booking/listing, last-message preview/time and unread count.
* Filters: all, unread, booking conversations, general enquiries and archived.
* Actions: open, search messages, mark unread, archive and report/block where policy permits.
* Only participants and authorised staff may access conversation data.
3.21 Conversation
ROUTE/dashboard/messages/:conversationIdPRESENTATIONFull page or conversation detail panePurpose: Display and manage an individual conversation.
* Participant identity/profile link and online/last-active state only if supported and privacy-compliant.
* Associated listing or booking; message history, timestamps, read state, composer, send action and validated attachments.
* Booking/listing summary links, report/block controls and participant-only access.
3.22 Payments & Earnings
ROUTE/dashboard/financePRESENTATIONFull page; Overview, Transactions, Earnings, Payouts and Payment Methods are tabsPurpose: Provide a consolidated view of financial activity.
* Summary: customer booking payments, provider earnings, pending provider earnings, payout-eligible balance, completed payouts and refunds where applicable.
* Finance sections: Overview, Transactions, Earnings, Payouts and Payment Methods.
* Recent transactions: reference, booking reference, type, amount, date, status and detail link.
* Explain customer payments, provider earnings, commission, pending settlement, completed payouts and refunds.
* All figures must derive from verified transaction/ledger records.
3.23 Payment Details
ROUTE/dashboard/finance/transactions/:transactionIdPRESENTATIONFull page or transaction detail drawerPurpose: Show one financial transaction.
* Transaction/gateway references, booking reference, type, amount/currency, date, method description if available, status, verification state, fees and refund status.
* Transaction timeline and booking link.
* Actions: view booking, download/print receipt if supported and contact support.
* Never display full card details or sensitive payment credentials.
3.24 Earnings
ROUTE/dashboard/finance/earningsPRESENTATIONFull page or Earnings tabPurpose: Show provider-side earnings.
* Gross provider revenue, TerraTills commission, other deductions, net earnings, pending earnings, payout-eligible earnings and paid amounts.
* Breakdown by listing/date; filters for date range, category, listing, booking and payout status.
* Link each earning to booking and payout records.
* Only show payout-eligible funds after completion, dispute and settlement conditions are satisfied.
3.25 Payouts
ROUTE/dashboard/finance/payoutsPRESENTATIONFull page or Payouts tab; confirmation may be modalPurpose: Show provider payout balances and history.
* Available, pending, completed and failed payout amounts; masked destination account; reference; initiation/completion dates; amount and status.
* Statuses: pending, processing, successful, failed, reversed and on hold.
* Allow user-initiated payout only if approved business rules and Bach capabilities support it.
* Verify and reconcile transfers; prevent duplicate payouts.
* Actions: view payout, update payout account where permitted and contact support.
3.26 Payment Methods
ROUTE/dashboard/finance/payment-methodsPRESENTATIONFull page or tab; add/edit may be modal/drawerPurpose: Manage customer payment methods and provider payout destinations.
* Separate customer payment methods from provider payout accounts.
* Show masked account details, account holder, bank, verification state and default status where relevant.
* Add/update/set default/remove only where provider capabilities and policy permit.
* Do not store or display sensitive payment credentials in frontend code.
* Bach is selected; verify its actual supported payment, settlement, transfer, refund, webhook and payout capabilities before enabling dependent functions.
3.27 Reviews
ROUTE/dashboard/reviewsPRESENTATIONFull page; Received, Given and Awaiting My Review are tabsPurpose: Show reviews received and written by the user.
* Received: reviewer, service, booking reference, rating, feedback, date and provider response if present.
* Given: service, provider, rating, comment, submission date and permitted edit/report actions.
* Awaiting: eligible completed booking, service, participant and Write Review action.
* Provide moderation and reporting.

* Show booking, service and person being reviewed.
* Collect overall rating and written review; optional category ratings for service quality, communication, reliability, listing accuracy, timeliness and satisfaction.
* Optional supporting image only if policy permits.
* Submit/cancel; enforce completed-booking eligibility, participant validation and duplicate-review rules.
3.29 Saved Items
ROUTE/dashboard/savedPRESENTATIONFull pagePurpose: Allow users to revisit bookmarked listings.
* Show listing image, title, category, provider, location, price, availability, rating if available and date saved.
* Search, category filter, sort by date/price, remove, share and open listing.
* Empty state links to Explore Services.
3.30 Notifications

* Categories: bookings, service requests, messages, payments, payouts, reviews, verification, security, support and announcements.
* Each notification contains title, summary, timestamp, read state, related record and destination.
* Actions: mark read, mark all read, filter, open related page and delete where policy permits.

3.33 Support Ticket Details

3.34 User Profile
ROUTE/dashboard/profilePRESENTATIONFull pagePurpose: Display the user's public-facing profile and account summary.

* Profile information: public name, biography, location, areas served, skills/experience and privacy-controlled contact details.
* Active services with category, price, location and listing links.
* Reputation: reviews and measurable completed-booking/provider indicators if TerraTills elects to display them.
* Actions: Edit Profile, Verification Centre, Settings and view public profile.
* Do not expose private home address, identity-document numbers, bank details or private contact information without authorised purpose and consent.
3.35 Edit Profile

* Identify public and private fields.
* Support save/cancel, image change and appropriate email/phone verification.
3.36 Verification Centre

* Service verification: authority to lease farmland, machinery ownership/authority, relevant machinery inspection, training credentials and other category-specific evidence.
* Statuses: not started, required, submitted, under review, approved, rejected, resubmission required and expired where applicable.
* Actions: start/continue, upload missing document, view requirements/history and contact support.
* Only require evidence relevant to the user's activity; booking a tractor does not require machinery ownership verification.
3.37 Verification Submission

* Identify verification type and explain why it is required; list accepted documents.
* Collect relevant details, secure uploads, declarations and consent.
* Show upload progress, submission confirmation and status.
* Restrict private documents to authorised verification staff and the submitting user as appropriate.
* Define secure storage, access logging, retention and deletion.
